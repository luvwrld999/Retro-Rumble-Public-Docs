FriLock Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

FriLock is an app for keeping files, photos, videos, music and notes in encrypted vaults on your device. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: FriLock does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

What FriLock stores, and where

  • Your vaults. Everything you add to a vault is encrypted on your device before it is written to storage. Vaults are stored either in FriLock's private app storage or in a folder on your device that you choose. FriLock never uploads them.
  • Settings on this device. Your theme, auto-lock choices, which vaults are pinned to the bottom bar and the labels you gave them, and where vaults are stored. These are kept in FriLock's private storage and never leave the device. They contain no vault contents, keys or passphrases.
  • Biometric and "remember on this phone" keys. If you turn these on, a key that opens the vault is protected by your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore). It cannot be exported, is not backed up, and is deleted when you turn the feature off, hard-lock the vault, or it expires.

What FriLock does not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No internet access: the app does not request the internet permission.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone or phone identifiers. FriLock does not ask for camera permission: when you take a photo or video into a vault, your camera app takes it and FriLock encrypts it straight away.
  • FriLock's developer cannot see, recover or reset your vaults, passphrases or keys.

Files you choose to share

FriLock only reads files you pick with the system file picker and only writes files where you choose to save them. When you share an encrypted file, mount a vault in the Files app, or export a backup, the destination app or location is chosen by you; what it does with data is governed by its own policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to show that a vault is mounted (with an Unmount button), to deliver mount reminders you set, and to show the progress of a transfer that continues while you use other apps.
  • Run at startup — to restore mount reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Biometrics — for optional fingerprint or face unlock. FriLock never receives your fingerprint or face data; Android only reports whether authentication succeeded.

Children

FriLock is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Data deletion

All FriLock data stays on your device. Deleting a vault in the app removes its encrypted files and every setting FriLock kept about it. Uninstalling FriLock removes app storage; vaults you kept in a folder you chose remain there until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
